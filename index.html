My Work

Online English Classes on Zoom

Learn English from the comfort of your home with live, interactive classes on Zoom. I conduct a small batch 1:1 sessions focused on speaking, grammer,writing, and exam preparation. Each class includes doubt clearing, notes, practice exercises, and regular feedback to track your progress. All you need is a phone or laptop and good internet. Join from anywhere in India or abroad.

Support on WhatsApp

Along with live classes, every students gets full support on WhatsApp. You will recieve daiy notes, grammer worksheet, vocabulary lists and practice questions directly on WhatsApp. You can also send your doubts, homework, and speaking recording anytime and get personal feedback from me. This ensures you stay connected and keep learning even after class.

IELTS/Spoken English Coaching

Crack the IELTS Speaking test with confidence through live online coaching on Zoom. I train students for all 3 parts of the speaking test with real exam-style practice, feedback, and band score evaluation . Classes focus on fluency, pronunciation, grammer, and vocabulary. You will also get model answers, cue card topics, and Personalized tips to improve from your currend band to your target band.
